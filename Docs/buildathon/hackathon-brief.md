## Four-step process

1. **Pick a track** — choose one of five AI tracks.
2. **Build something real** — working prototype, not theory.
3. **Show your work** — public repo, 5-minute pitch video, architecture.
4. **Panel** — if the build has signal, Razorpay calls you in.

## Tracks

### 01 — AI Growth & Agentic Commerce

Grow the merchant's revenue, and make them sellable to AI buyers.

Build an agent that grows revenue for a merchant on Razorpay test-mode APIs, or that makes a
merchant transactable by an AI buyer end to end.

**Why now:** NPCI's UAP and the global protocol race (ACP, AP2, x402) make agent-to-agent commerce
the open problem of the year, and Razorpay's in-app pilots are already live.

**Example directions:** Conversational in-app checkout, Agent-readable catalog, Upsell & cross-sell
agent, Campaign orchestrator.

**The bar:** Every money action explainable, bounded and gated. Show the audit trail and one failure
handled gracefully.

### 02 — AI Risk Manager

Stop the merchant losing money to fraud, returns and chargebacks. Build a working detector, verifier
or auto-responder for one class of loss, with measured precision and recall on a held-out test set.

**The bar:** Honest metrics including false-positive cost. Strictly defense-only.

### 03 — AI Revenue Recovery

Find revenue that's slipping away and win it back. Build an agent that detects revenue at risk,
determines the right intervention, and executes a bounded recovery workflow.

**The bar:** Measured money recovered across a batch, with compliant escalation, stopping rules, and
an audit trail.

### 04 — AI Finance Controller

Run the books and the cash position. Build an agent that closes one finance-ops loop across a 50+
record batch of synthetic data.

**The bar:** Throughput plus measured accuracy plus an honest exception list.

### 05 — Open Track

Build what you believe should exist. Any domain, workflow, or user.

**The bar:** Real problem, working product, meaningful use of AI, evidence of value.

## Judging criteria

From the official page and secondary analysis:

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Problem taste | Selecting a meaningful, real-world financial or merchant problem |
| Build quality | Clean repository structure, execution reliability, and code trust |
| AI judgment | Using AI models appropriately; deterministic solutions where AI is unnecessary |
| Failure recovery | What broke during development and how it was resolved |
